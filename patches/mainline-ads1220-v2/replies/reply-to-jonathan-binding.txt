Thanks for the pointer. I looked at how adi,ad4130 and adi,ad4170-4
model this (per-channel adi,excitation-current-*-microamp properties
plus pin routing); the ADS1220's IDAC1/IDAC2 routing would map onto the
same shape, and the low-side bridge switch could be a boolean. I have
kept these out of scope for v2 to keep the initial driver reviewable -
nothing in the current binding prevents adding them compatibly later.
Happy to extend the binding now instead if you prefer it complete up
front.

Done in v2.

> Why 6?  Is the idea that you might have both differential and single ended
> inputs from the same physical wires, or overlapping differential
> pairs? In practice that rarely reflects how boards are wired.
> However I can't immediately figure out a combination that ends up with 7 channels.

You are right, the 7 was an oversight - there is no combination that
gives exactly 7. The multiplexer supports 12 input configurations
(4 single-ended + 8 differential pairs), so v2 raises the channel@
pattern and reg maximum to 0..11. The overlapping-pairs case (e.g.
AIN0-AIN1 and AIN1-AIN2) is indeed why the count can exceed 4.
