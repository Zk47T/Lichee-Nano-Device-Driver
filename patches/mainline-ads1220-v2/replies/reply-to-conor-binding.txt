> Maybe this is a silly question, but what is the difference between 3,2
> and 2,3? Which pin is considered the positive value?

Not silly at all - it was undocumented. The first cell is the positive
PGA input (AINP) and the second the negative input (AINN). The ADS1220
multiplexer can route AIN1/AIN0 and AIN3/AIN2 with swapped polarity
(MUX codes 0x6 and 0x7), which is why those two reversed pairs - and
only those two - appear in the list. v2 documents this in the
diff-channels description.
